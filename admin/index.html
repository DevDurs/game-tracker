Recent Results

Off by default. PandaScore's free tier doesn't include past-match results — that's the paid "Historical Data" plan, priced per videogame (from roughly £400/month, per their pricing page). With this off, the dashboard skips that request entirely rather than spending a call every refresh on data that mostly comes back empty. Turning it on will still show something for high-match-volume titles (matches that drop off "Live" are held onto locally for a while even without the paid plan), but for lower-volume titles expect it to mostly stay empty unless you've actually upgraded that game's PandaScore plan.
